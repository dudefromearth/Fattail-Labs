# Amendment AZ-VP-9-A11 — Purpose, Workflow Modes, and the Position Layer

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Authority:** Coach directive (reconciliation session): the app will
be used for the morning routine, for trade entry, and for trade
management.
**Extends:** A2–A10. Nothing struck.

## The purpose law

1. **Three uses govern the design:** MORNING ROUTINE (read the
   terrain before the open), TRADE ENTRY (place the structure
   against the levels), TRADE MANAGEMENT (hold/adjust/exit against
   the levels). Every surface decision is judged against these
   three; a feature serving none of them fails A7 by definition.

## Workflow modes (presets)

2. **Three named modes ship as one-click presets** — MORNING
   ROUTINE, TRADE ENTRY, TRADE MANAGEMENT — each a saved
   configuration of layers, opacity, analysis on/off, timeframe,
   and span. House defaults for each are Coach-tuned from the
   screen (his show configuration is the Morning Routine preset,
   so members can match his chart in one click); the member's own
   adjustments save per mode; reset-to-house-default always one
   action away.
3. First run opens in Morning Routine with a one-time pointer to
   the analysis toggle; thereafter the A2 default-off law governs.

## Reading instruments (serve all three uses)

4. **Span chip:** always visible, states the profile's aggregation
   span and coverage honestly ("Profile: Sep 17–18" / "Full
   history" / truncation per Contract v1.1); it is the click
   target for the x-range dialog (A10).
5. **Crosshair readout:** at the cursor's price — bin volume, and
   with the overlay on, the nearest structural line with its class
   (node floor / ceiling / intranode / internodal). The bridge
   from seeing terrain to reading it; core VP function within A7.
6. **Palette separation:** structure owns the saturated green/red
   (A3). The default candle theme uses muted/hollow tones so price
   candles never collide with node boundaries at the same pixels;
   candle theme is config, the separation principle is law.

## The Position Layer (trade entry & management)

7. **L-POSITION is a named future layer** (A8 L4+ family): the
   member's active defined-risk structure — strikes, wings,
   breakevens — rendered against the terrain, data from the
   platform's position/Analyzer services when its gate opens.
   Until then it appears per SA-L11 as an honest in-development
   entry. Rendering will follow a dedicated encoding amendment;
   it never borrows structure's colors (clause 6 applies to it
   in advance).

## Standing

Citable law until folded into the SA spec's next authored version.
Surface work binds to A11 from this date.
