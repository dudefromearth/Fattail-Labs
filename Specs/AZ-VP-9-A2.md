# Amendment AZ-VP-9-A2 — Member Volume Profile Surface

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Authority:** Coach directive (reconciliation session, verbatim intent):
the primary view must be the actual Volume Profile; any structural
analysis is an overlay, off by default, requiring the user to switch
on; price scale left by default, capable of left/right/both; the
profile capable of left/right orientation.
**Supersedes:** AZ-VP-9-A1 in part — its objects-only member-surface
clause is struck. Its route fate (this surface replaces the residual
client-binned canvas at ship, which then dies under the VP §2 kill
rule) and the original "no candlesticks" clause carry forward
unchanged.

## The surface law (AZ-VP-9 as amended, A2)

1. **Primary view = the volume-by-price histogram** (the actual
   Volume Profile), rendered from Volume Profile Service bins at
   display resolution (render-only rebinning permitted per SA-L8;
   detection input unaffected).
2. **Structural Analysis is an overlay, DEFAULT OFF.** The user
   switches it on. When off, no SA objects render. When on, SA
   objects draw over the profile per SA spec §8 encoding (SA-L10
   neutral, location legend). The overlay toggle is prominent and
   honest — never pre-enabled, never sticky-on by deployment.
3. **Price axis:** left by default; user-configurable to left,
   right, or both.
4. **Profile orientation:** bars anchored to the left edge growing
   rightward, or anchored right growing leftward — user-selectable.
5. Provenance (source → target, mapping badge, coverage floor),
   states (GAPPED / STALE / NO COVERAGE), and the verbatim caption
   remain mandatory on the surface. Chrome budget law stands (chart
   owns the viewport).
6. **Bins delivery:** member browsers never hold computing-class
   credentials; bins reach the member surface through the
   authenticated Labs backend (display path). The VP API's direct-
   access deny for member-class tokens stands; VP-L18 is read as
   amended by this clause — raw bins may render on the member VP
   surface via the Labs display path.
7. **Licensing note (Stage B, standing precondition):** member
   display of SPY→XSP profiles sits within the existing equities
   entitlement posture. Member display of ES→SPX fine-resolution
   profiles re-engages the recorded Massive written-confirmation
   precondition before member ship; until resolved, ES→SPX member
   display may require coarsened resolution — an engine display
   parameter, not a redesign.

## Standing

This record is the citable member-surface law until the SA and VP
specs fold it into their next authored versions, whose change tables
cite this file. Surface work binds to A2 from this date; anything
citing A1's objects-only clause is citing superseded law.
