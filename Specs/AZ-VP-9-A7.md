# Amendment AZ-VP-9-A7 — Charting Scope Boundary

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Authority:** Coach directive (reconciliation session, verbatim
intent): we recreate in large part the TradingView charting, but
LIMITED to the features specified for the full implementation of the
VP tool.
**Extends:** A2–A6. Nothing struck; this bounds them.

## The scope law

1. **The surface delivers TradingView-class chart mechanics** — the
   pan/zoom/axes/crosshair familiarity members already have — via
   the A5 engine, in service of the VP tool. The chart serves the
   profile; the profile is the product.
2. **The feature set is CLOSED over the specified laws:** the
   profile and its orientation/axis options (A2), the color grammar
   and overlay (A3), axes, grid, and range controls (A4), the price
   layer in line/candle/bar at the specified timeframes (A6),
   provenance/states/caption, the SA-L11 in-development navigation,
   and the phases the SA spec names (Exploration, Replay, Footprint,
   GEX overlay, Characterization) when their gates open.
3. **Explicitly OUT unless a future amendment laws them in:** user
   drawing tools, general indicator libraries, alerts (separate
   product, standing law), watchlists, multi-chart layouts, symbol
   search beyond the product's instruments, social/publishing
   features, and any TradingView capability not named in A2–A6 or
   the SA spec.
4. **Additions go through the loop:** a wanted feature becomes a
   Coach directive → authored amendment → build. "TradingView has
   it" is never, by itself, a reason to build it.
5. Effort rule for the builders: when a choice arises between
   deepening chart generality and completing a specified VP
   feature, the specified feature wins — scope creep toward a
   general charting platform is a finding, not initiative.

## Standing

Citable scope law until folded into the SA spec's next authored
version. All surface work binds to A7 from this date.
