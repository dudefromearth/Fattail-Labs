## 1. The derivation rule — general, not Factory-specific

**Every publishable thing has a wiki page derived from it, and the wiki page is
always Oscar's.**

The source system owns the artifact. Oscar owns the corpus entry.

| The thing | Its corpus entry |
|---|---|
| A course | the course wiki page |
| A help guide | the help wiki page |
| A Knowledge app | its wiki page |
| A product | its wiki page |

> "A course isn't the course wiki." — Coach

The two are never the same object. Same source, different surface, different reader.

**This rule already existed.** The Factory needed no exception. The advisor built one
anyway — Gemba authoring the wiki page — and it took several hours and a Wiki-side
contradiction to surface that the general rule already covered it.

## 2. Naming — Knowledge app and Intelligence app

**Coined by Coach this session.** A **Knowledge app** is what was previously called a
template. **Intelligence apps** are the tier above.

"Template" undersells it — a template sounds like a starting point you fill in; a
Knowledge app is a thing someone uses.

**Reach, flagged not actioned.** This is a platform-wide term, not a Factory one.
"Template" appears throughout the Factory spec, the IKI hierarchy documents, the store,
and the pricing structure. **OPEN — Coach:** working name, or the real one? Nothing
has been propagated.

**Possible collision:** Options Lab and Strategy Lab are also "apps." Whether
"Intelligence app" reads cleanly against those is undirected.

## 3. The zygote claim

> "Intelligence apps evolve from similar zygotes." — Coach

## 4. Factory changes ruled tonight — for the spec and the charter

### 4.1 The wiki page leaves Gemba's scope

**Reverses part of DL-580**, which gave Gemba both the help page and the wiki page.
Needs its own decision-log entry — not a quiet edit. Same discipline as the
invariant 9 catch earlier tonight.

**Gemba's Staged list is four artifacts:**

- the product
- the landing page draft
- the store placement
- **the help guide**

The wiki page is Oscar's, composed from the publication signal.

### 4.2 Help guide vs. help wiki page — two artifacts, not one

| Artifact | Surface | Author |
|---|---|---|
| **Help guide** | served in-app by the Help bot | **Gemba** |
| **Help wiki page** | the corpus | **Oscar**, derived from the published help guide |

**The source material for the wiki page is the published help guide, not the spec.**
Which is why Gemba does not need to write it — the thing Oscar composes from already
exists.

**Spec ambiguity to fix:** §7.3 and §7.6 both say "help documentation" and "wiki page"
without distinguishing which surface either lives on. That ambiguity is probably part
of how §7.6 ended up contradicting itself.

### 4.3 §7.6 changes — publish is not conditional on a wiki page

**Was:** a wiki page is part of what a product is; nothing goes Live without one.

**Now:** the product publishes on Coach's switch **whether or not a wiki page exists**.
It then becomes **visibly noisy** — flagged as missing its wiki page — and stays noisy
until one exists.

On publication it **notifies Oscar** exactly as a course or a help guide does. Same
signal, same path, no special case for the Factory, no second write path, no envelope.

**OPEN — Coach:** what "noisy" looks like. On the card, on the product, or both. This
is the mechanism that makes the design work rather than leaving a missing page nobody
notices.

### 4.4 The Wiki-side contradiction dissolves

Wiki Spec §7 (board approval) versus §11 (no agent-direct publish, no second approval
state) was blocking IF-8's wiki-page artifact.

**It no longer applies.** Nothing publishes into the Wiki off the Factory's switch.
Oscar composes on his own board, through his own single write path, on his own
schedule. The Wiki program still has its internal question, but the **Factory no
longer depends on the answer.**

### 4.5 IF-8 scope corrected

The advisor's IF-8 prompt excluded the wiki page pending the Wiki program's answer.
**That exclusion is now permanent and for a different reason** — the wiki page was
never the Factory's to build.

IF-8 builds four artifacts. Nothing waits on the Wiki program.
