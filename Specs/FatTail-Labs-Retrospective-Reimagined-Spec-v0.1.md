## 1. What is dead

**The nine-step ceremony is dead** — both as **chrome** (the 3×3 map, fixed walk, tiles as
the experience) and as **Coach conduct** (the Coach does not walk members through those
nine steps).

v0.7.1 §6 (fixed sequence, steps 1–9) is **not** the future experience. It remains
as-built until this reimagining is approved and replaces it. It is not the vision.
