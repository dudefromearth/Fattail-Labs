## 1. Coach's ruling and rationale

The red↔green money gradient on the Journal calendar is **ratified — as a mental-toughness instrument.** Rationale, in Coach's frame: traders naturally reject the sight of loss, and the not-looking is where avoidable loss compounds. The calendar is deliberate **exposure**: the member is confronted with the thing they would naturally avoid and learns to accept it. Softening the stimulus (single-hue) would be the platform flinching on the member's behalf — a capacity-over-dependency violation of its own kind. The gradient, the P&L amount, and **R:R** together drive the lesson home: R:R is the interpretive number that teaches the eye to read a red day at designed R:R as *process working*, not verdict.

## 2. The scoped exception (register law, amended)

| Surface family | Valence color |
|---|---|
| Capital, positions, blotter, Reports | **Forbidden** (unchanged — color there is decoration) |
| **Journal calendar equity map** | **Sanctioned** — full red/green gradient (color here is curriculum) |

**Gradient conditionality (Coach, 2026-08-09):** ratification is **conditional on the gradient** — magnitude-scaled color is the instrument. Solid binary red/green blocks would be a verdict scoreboard and are **rejected** ("if they were all solid colors I might not go for it"). The gradient must be **obvious** — clearly perceptible magnitude steps, not a subtle wash: a $30 give-back and a $3,000 bleed must read differently at a glance. Consequence for §5.4: intensity normalization must be **stable across periods** (fixed buckets or capital-relative — not period-max), so a quiet month of small nets never paints itself dramatic; the exposure only teaches if intensity tells the truth month over month.

The two specs cite each other; the boundary is doctrine, not accident. Lima logs the carve-out with the mental-toughness rationale.

## 3. The toggle (Coach-ratified requirement: "maybe you could turn it on/off")

The on/off control is **part of the ratification**, not an optional nicety — exposure must be **chosen, not ambient**. Chosen looking is training; ambush is flooding, and flooding breeds avoidance of the Journal itself.

- Persistent per-member setting on the Journal calendar: **Equity map on/off**. State survives sessions.
- **Default:** [OPEN — Coach one-word disposition] Advisor recommendation, labeled opinion: **default ON** — exposure as the practice, the off switch as the mercy. Default OFF is the gentler onboarding. One word decides.
- Off state: calendar reverts to current activity chrome (no amounts, no gradient, no period bar). **No residual money chrome — off means off.**
- The toggle is calendar chrome only; CJ-6 untouched — chat, prompts, and notifications never speak money in any state.
