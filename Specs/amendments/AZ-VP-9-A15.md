# Amendment AZ-VP-9-A15 — Annotated Benchmark Verdicts (Round 2)

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Authority:** Coach's annotated TV screenshots (2026-09-18, second
set) — filed with this amendment as benchmark round 2.
**Extends:** A13/A14 (look and fonts). Refines; nothing struck.

## The verdicts, as law

1. **Fonts MUCH BIGGER — TV size.** Axis figures at the benchmark's
   size (~12–13 px equivalent, matching the annotated screenshot),
   not the current small text; same scale-up for the time axis and
   chips. "Reads at a glance from show distance" is the test.
2. **Candle detail — bordered style:** body OUTLINE and WICK in a
   slightly darker shade of the candle's color; body FILL in the
   lighter shade (TV's bordered candles). Purpose: candles stay
   legible when compressed. Border/fill shades are theme values
   Coach tunes.
3. **Time axis bigger and RESPONSIVE:** larger labels per verdict 1,
   and tick content adapts to the visible span (dates at wide
   spans, session days + hours mid-zoom, minutes intraday) with
   density that breathes as the span changes — never a fixed
   sparse row.
4. **Fill top-to-bottom with padding:** the y-range fits the
   visible data span stretched to the full canvas height with
   ~75 px padding top and bottom (Coach's annotated number; theme
   value). No dead vertical bands beyond the padding.
5. **Price scale in FUTURES INCREMENTS, responsive:** tick values
   land on the instrument's real grid — 0.25 minimums, with major
   labels on clean futures rounds (2.5 / 5 / 10 / 25-point majors
   as zoom dictates) — never arbitrary decimals like 7687.11.
   Label density adapts to zoom. Grid lines follow the same
   increments (A4.3).
