# Amendment AZ-VP-9-A17 — Behavioral Parity Default

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Authority:** Coach directive (verbatim frustration, recorded as
law): the surface must act like every other charting system in the
world without item-by-item dictation.
**Supersedes:** A4/A11's default x-window ("current session span") —
that default produced a mostly-blank future canvas and is struck.
**Extends:** A5/A13 into behavior: the benchmark governs conduct,
not just looks.

## The parity law

1. **TV behavior is the DEFAULT for everything.** The A5 engine's
   native TradingView-class behaviors ship enabled and configured
   to TV-standard defaults. A deviation from how TradingView
   behaves is a DEFECT unless a numbered law explicitly requires
   the deviation. The burden of proof flips: the builder proves
   parity; Coach never dictates standard behavior again.
2. **Default visible window (fixes the struck default):** a
   trailing window of recent bars ending at NOW with a small
   right offset (a few bars of future whitespace, TV-standard) —
   the data fills the canvas. Session-span and full-history views
   are one click away (range presets / span chip), never the
   blank-future default.
3. **Named defects, fixed under this law:** (a) canvas mostly
   blank with data crammed left; (b) dragging the price scale
   loses the series into blank space; (c) no recovery affordance.
4. **Parity checklist (minimum, proven with evidence, not asked
   about):** content fills the view on open; scroll-wheel zoom
   about the cursor; drag-pan both axes with the series never
   lost (pan bounds keep data reachable); axis-drag rescales
   about the visible data; DOUBLE-CLICK an axis resets it to
   auto-fit; double-click canvas / reset control returns to the
   default view; autoscale keeps series visible unless the user
   explicitly locks scale; kinetic/momentum feel per the engine's
   defaults; crosshair tracking per TV. Anything on this list
   not working is a defect today, not a future item.
5. Standing acceptance: before any look/behavior report reaches
   Coach's eye, the builder attests the checklist against the
   benchmark side-by-side and lists any intentional deviation
   with its authorizing law number.

## Standing

Citable law until folded into the SA spec's next authored version.
All surface work binds to A17 from this date.
