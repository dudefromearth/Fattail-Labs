# Amendment AZ-VP-9-A8 — Layer Architecture

**Date:** 2026-09-18
**Authority:** Coach directive (reconciliation session, verbatim
intent): a familiar backdrop as the canvas, with similar VP tools,
and a standard price chart on a separate layer from the VP and
analysis; Footprint, when reached, is another layer.
**Extends:** A2–A7; consolidates their elements into an explicit
layer model. Nothing struck.

## The layer law

1. **The surface is ONE canvas of independent layers** on the
   familiar backdrop (A5 engine; A4 axes/grid; A7 scope):
   - L0 BACKDROP — black canvas, dark gray grid, axes, ranges (A4).
   - L1 PRICE CHART — standard price series, line/candle/bar,
     timeframes per A6. Independent layer; default ON.
   - L2 VOLUME PROFILE — blue side-anchored histogram (A2/A3);
     the product's identity layer; default ON.
   - L3 STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS — the colored line grammar (A3);
     default OFF per A2; user switches on.
   - L4+ FUTURE LAYERS — canvas-native phases arrive as layers
     here when their gates open: FOOTPRINT is the first named
     (its own layer); GEX overlay likewise when reached.
     Genuinely distinct experiences (Replay) remain views; the
     SA-L11 navigation continues to show every phase honestly
     either way.
2. **Independence:** each layer toggles on/off without affecting
   the others; layer visibility, format, and settings persist per
   user. A compact layer control lives within the chrome budget.
3. **Z-order (back to front):** L0, L1, L2, L3, L4+ — price behind
   the profile, structure lines in front spanning the canvas
   (the core read: price against structure), future layers
   ordered by law when introduced.
4. **Coherence:** all layers on the canvas render the same
   instrument and the same x-window (A4) — one symbol, one span,
   many lenses; never mixed spans on one canvas.
5. Layer additions follow A7 clause 4: directive → amendment →
   build. No layer ships pre-enabled that the law says defaults
   off.

## Standing

Citable architecture law until folded into the SA spec's next
authored version. All surface work binds to A8 from this date.
