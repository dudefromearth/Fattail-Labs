# W0-3 — Tango vocabulary

**Status:** PASS  
**Date:** 2026-08-09  

## Register

**Quiet umpire:** small, neutral, factual. Same voice as campaign variance lines. No amber/red shame, no “you should,” no profit language.

## Approved phrases

### Undirected (lawful rest)

| Surface | Copy |
|---------|------|
| Picker empty | **No campaign** |
| Blotter | (no chip) — never “Unassigned” / “Missing campaign” |
| Help / empty | “Trades without a campaign stay on the account only. Direct a season when you want structure.” |
| Filter | **Undirected** (not “Unaffiliated campaign”) |

### Fund / defund

| Action | Label |
|--------|--------|
| Deposit | **Fund** / “Add funds” |
| Withdrawal | **Withdraw** / “Remove funds” |
| Note placeholder | “Optional note (e.g. transfer, tuition)” — never “reason for spending” |
| Reversal | **Correct with reverse entry** — not “delete mistake” |

### Overcommit (quiet)

- “Declared allocations sum to {pct}% of total capital.”  
- “{Campaign} claims {amt} from {Account}; that account holds {bal}.”  
Never: “Overlevered,” “violation,” “breach,” “risk limit exceeded.”

### Master drawdown (quiet)

- “Trading drawdown {realized_$} vs budget {budget_$} ({tol}% of capital).”  
Never: “You failed your drawdown limit.”

### Staleness (two-clock)

- “as of {N} days / {M} fills ago”  
- Buying power self-report: “Buying power {x} — as of …”  
- Confirm: **Confirm as current** (refreshes as-of only)  
- Honesty prompt: “Balances last confirmed {ago}. Confirm as current?”  
Never: “Stale data,” “outdated risk,” red aging.

### Arbitrary BP posture

- “Working buying power (not tracked against a broker).”  
No aging line.

## Ban list

violated · unlock · reward · earn access · failed · bad trader · missing structure · must assign campaign · profit · P&L hero · “stop trading”  

## Ledger language

Do not reintroduce “ledger” / “default campaign” as member-facing furniture. Historical internal migration notes may say furniture/ledger.
