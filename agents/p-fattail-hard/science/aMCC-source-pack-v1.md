### Abstract-grounded summary (read: PubMed abstract)

- Paper term **tenacity** = persistence in the face of challenge; product term = **mental toughness**.  
- Linked in the review’s framing to academic, career, and health outcomes (product may **not** convert that into profit claims).  
- **aMCC** (anterior mid-cingulate cortex) is proposed as an important **network hub** that performs **cost/benefit computations** for that capacity.  
- Position as structural/functional hub allows integration of signals to predict energy needs for attention, encoding, and movement in service of **goal attainment**.  
- Review integrates attention, reward, memory, affect, multimodal sensory integration, motor control.  
- Keywords (paper): Anterior mid-cingulate cortex; Energy regulation; Tenacity.

### Product-safe claim lines (use with full cite)

1. “Research reviews the **anterior mid-cingulate cortex (aMCC)** as a hub linked to **mental toughness** — persisting when challenge and effort are costly (Touroutoglou et al., 2020, *Cortex*).”  
2. “FatTail Hard is built as **repeated voluntary challenge** so you practice mental toughness under load — the capacity that aMCC literature associates with willpower and persistence.”  
3. “Your Mental Toughness meter tracks **compliance with the challenge you chose** — a process signal, not a brain scan.”
