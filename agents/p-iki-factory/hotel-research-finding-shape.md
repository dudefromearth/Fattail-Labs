# Hotel — Research finding shape (IF-2)

Not a skill. Not advice. Not a profit claim.

| Field | Law |
|-------|-----|
| `title` | What was found. Noun phrase. No “will make money.” |
| `rank` | Integer 1…n as produced. Do not invent ranks to fill 10. |
| `reason` | Why this rank, from sources. Process / evidence. **No** advice (“buy”, “you should”). |
| `sources` | List of evidence refs (URLs, doc paths, quotes). Empty sources → not usable. |

**Forbidden in any field:** profit claims, expected return, fill promises, Invariant #8 violations.

Unusable findings are dropped, not padded. Fewer than 10 is a pass.
