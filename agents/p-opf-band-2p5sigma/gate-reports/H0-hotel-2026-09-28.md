## 1. T_σ = max(trading_dte, 1)

Same floor Hotel already signed as **OC5a**: √T never uses 0. `trading_dte` as today (weekdays in `(session_day, exp]`; holidays counted — slightly wider).

| Book | `trading_dte` | `T_σ` | Year fraction |
|------|---------------|-------|---------------|
| 0DTE | 0 | **1** | 1/252 |
| 1DTE | 1 | 1 | 1/252 |
| 2–5 DTE | 2–5 | 2–5 | T/252 |

**0DTE.** Without the floor, `√(0/252) = 0` and the band collapses onto ATM as the clock runs out. That is the opposite of capture. Flooring at one trading day overstates remaining hours after the open; overstatement **widens**. Remaining-hours decay would **narrow** through the session — unsafe for this job. The ratchet (`active(t)` this session) already keeps morning strikes; `T_σ = 1` does not fight it.

**1–5 DTE.** The `max` binds only at 0. `T_σ = trading_dte` is the 252-day convention the IV is quoted in. Weekend variance on a Friday 1DTE (Monday expiry, `T_σ = 1`) lives in **that book’s ATM IV**, not in calendar T. Putting calendar-3 against weekend-inflated IV would double-count. Trading-day T + the book’s own IV is the split.

**0DTE and 1DTE share T_σ = 1.** Coarse, same as OC5a. Not a collapse: Monday 10:30 SPX 0DTE IV 11.1% vs 1DTE 11.7% (analysis). Lead 0.25σ sits on top. Not a finding.

Checked 0 through 5. Floor is necessary at 0; it does not starve 2–5.

## 2. Degenerate IV — hold last good, never collapse

Spec §1: book’s own ATM IV at that capture. If missing or degenerate (`<1%` or `>200%`), **hold the last good window for that book and log it**.

**`<1%`.** Friday 2026-09-25 SPX 0DTE ATM IV **0.8%** after the close is the exhibit (analysis: settled/garbage; σ_T **3.91 pt** at 7743). A live RTH SPX/XSP 0–5 DTE ATM is not below 1% (Monday 0DTE 11.1%). Using 0.8% as σ input would paint a ~±10 pt window and lie that 2.5σ was captured. Hold last **good** window — do not update last-good from a degenerate print.

**`>200%`.** Garbage rail (scale error, leftover percent vs decimal). Index 0–5 DTE has printed 100%+ on crash 0DTE; 200% is beyond that. Holding last good beats exploding the ladder. `wings_max` 4.0σ is the other rail. Thresholds confirmed; not a finding.

Hold is per **window** (admitted strikes), not a licence to recompute σ from the bad IV and then freeze that collapsed range as “good.”

## 3. Freeze path — Friday 0.8% into the next session / Tuesday

**Asked:** would hold-last-good freeze the stale 0.8% Friday close IV into the **next session’s** band (Monday) or **Tuesday’s** band (plan evidence day 2026-09-29)?

**No. Not a finding.** Do not invent a fourth rule.

Walk:

1. **0.8% is degenerate** (`<1%`). It **never becomes last-good**. Hold uses the previous good window; a print that fails the gate cannot seed the next one.
2. **Book is `(symbol, expiry)`** (spec §1). Friday 0DTE is `(SPX, 2026-09-25)`. That expiry is dead Monday. Monday 0DTE is `(SPX, 2026-09-28)` — Friday’s 1DTE, ATM IV **8.8%**, not degenerate. Tuesday 0DTE is `(SPX, 2026-09-29)` — Friday’s 2DTE, IV **8.3%**. The 0.8% print has no book to live in after Friday.
3. **Ratchet is this session** (`earlier this session for this book`). It does not carry a Friday window into Monday.

Friday 0DTE also had **no RTH last-good** (collector down; four snaps after 16:00). That is spec §5 / plan “day missed,” not this hold rule, and it does not propagate. Cold-start with no last-good on an expired book is not a next-session freeze. Hotel does not add a default IV.

If Alpha keyed last-good by **DTE slot** or process-global SPX IV, or admitted a degenerate print as last-good, that is a **W1 defect vs §1**, not a spec change. K1 already names “degenerate IV holds last good.” Scope of the existing rule: last-good is `(symbol, expiry)`, session-bounded, and a `<1%` / `>200%` print never writes it.
