# Knowledge Check — Journey & Process Flow

*FatTail Labs OS · Module 2 — The Journey*

Six questions. Open this quiz lesson in the course player, answer all items, and submit for immediate scoring.

---

## Questions

**1.** What is **Journey** primarily for?

- A. A second login for payments and broker links
- B. A cross-Lab view of your path and Process Flow across courses, live, and practice
- C. A public ranking of who made the most money this month
- D. A replacement for Trade Log and Journal

---

**2.** Process Flow is best described as:

- A. The grade you got after a one-time exam
- B. A state of being — whether your trading process is coherent and current (a practice compass)
- C. Your account equity curve
- D. A peer-visible profit score

---

**3.** True or false: Process Flow should treat long calendar gaps (e.g. time away from the market) the same as failing process while you are actively trading.

- A. True — every quiet week should lower your standing equally
- B. False — silence outside an engaged stretch is not the same as dropping journals/retros while still operating

---

**4.** What should **never** move Process Flow?

- A. Retrospective cadence and plan adherence on tagged trades
- B. Learning rhythm and live presence while engaged
- C. P&L, win rate, and peer profit ranking
- D. Journal coverage on days you actually trade

---

**5.** You open Journey and see quiz results and “continue learning.” What are those for?

- A. Gamifying P&L so winners climb a ladder
- B. Tracking understanding and course progress so you can resume the next unfinished lesson
- C. Replacing the need to journal after trades
- D. Publishing your answers to the community board

---

**6.** A new member’s Process Flow pillars look empty. The best reading is:

- A. They failed and should feel ranked “Poor” immediately
- B. Empty means not started / establishing — not moral failure
- C. The system is broken
- D. They must enroll in Toughness before Journey works

---

## Answers

**1 — B.**

Journey is the honest dashboard for path + Process Flow across the Lab — not a profit board and not a substitute for Practice tools.

---

**2 — B.**

Process Flow is directional alignment with practice (compass / radar), not a report-card score or P&L.

---

**3 — B.**

Active-span logic: off-market quiet is not failure. Dropping journals, retros, or plan-following *while engaged* is what should show up.

---

**4 — C.**

Process only. Money outcomes and profit ranks are banned from Process Flow.

---

**5 — B.**

Learning path + quizzes check understanding and help you resume — they are not a profit game.

---

**6 — B.**

Empty ≠ failure. New members establish over time; Toughness is optional.
