# Six Weeks — Program Outline (v0.1)

2026-10-05 · Coach

One outcome: a paid Observer finishes six weeks trained, in a daily routine, and chooses Navigator. First draft for Coach to edit; the Navigator introduction moves to week 4, one week earlier than the current spec.

## Week 1 — Orientation: lift the cloud

Goal: they know what this is, where everything is, and they've committed.

- Reframe on day one: this is six weeks of training, not a trial. Here's who you'll be at day 42.
- Commitment form (Gate 1), then pledge posted in #introductions
- Complete the Start Here coursework
- Map of the house: where the courses are, the live-stream archive, how to join the 11:00–12:30 ET coach call, the FatTail trade channel and how trades are posted there
- Introduce the app: what it's for, first login, one guided walkthrough
- First Discord engagement: intro post, first daily takeaway
- Signal: commitment submitted, course started, first Discord post, call attended or replay watched

## Week 2 — Start the routine

Goal: they run the daily loop for the first time, with scaffolding.

- The daily loop is three steps: analyze in the morning, execute and manage during the day, journal throughout. Journaling is the thread, not a fourth step.
- No new tools. The journal app in Labs is the routine sheet: a free-form chat that timestamps every entry, accepts screenshots, and attaches the day's trades automatically. Nothing is pushed from the AI; the member opens the conversation.
- Taught routine: one journal entry before the open (the read and the plan), the trade logged from the Analyzer or the trade log during the day, one entry after the close (what happened, did I follow the plan, one takeaway)
- First butterfly placed by the rules: width by volatility, debit ≤ 10% of width, positioned so price runs into it (paper or small size)
- Training delivered this week: how to use the journal and the trade log, shown on the coach call and in a short walkthrough
- Daily takeaway in Discord continues
- End of week: first retrospective in the retrospective app. It reads every trade and journal entry since the last retrospective; with none before, it becomes the baseline. Friday ritual.
- Weekly check-in message: here's what you did, here's what's next
- Signal: the Journey. It runs continuously in Labs, tracks journal entries, trades, and retrospectives, and shows a radar map with a running tally. Week 2 target: entries on 3+ days, trades attached, baseline retrospective done (Gate 2)

## Week 3 — Second routine, second retrospective

Goal: first comparison. Two data points show direction.

- Script tone this week: encourage. Stick to the routine; you're close to the point where it sticks on its own. The habit is nearly formed, don't break the chain now.
- Routine runs unassisted; coaching focuses on management (100–200% runners, not the tent)
- Second retrospective, side by side with the first: what moved, what didn't
- Introduce expectancy and distributions in plain language: positioning, not prediction
- Retro closes on: what you did, where you stand, what week 4 asks
- Signal: Journey shows entries on 4+ days, retrospective 2 done

## Week 4 — Third retrospective, trends appear

Goal: they see their own behavior over time. First mention of what comes after.

- Script tone from here through week 6: see what you've built. The payoff of practicing fundamentals and managing your habits is now visible in your own Journey.
- Third retrospective; the Journey radar now shows three points of trend across every tracked behavior
- Coaching on behavior patterns, not just trades
- Make the coach relationship explicit: the scripts encourage the member to show up in the live coach call and in Discord, ask questions, and share their own experiences and trades if they want to. That interaction is part of the routine, not optional
- Soft sell: the value of coaching and group coaching in general, how other members have benefited from having someone on their side keeping them focused, and a heads-up that an offer to join the group is coming. No price yet (Gate 3)
- Retro closes on: what you did, where you stand, what weeks 5–6 ask
- Signal: retrospective 3 done. Call and Discord participation shows up in the member's own Journey radar; they measure themselves by looking at it, and the scripts point to it. The coach knows where people are but does not actively monitor

## Week 5 — Conversion campaign begins (indirect)

Goal: make the difference between coached and uncoached felt.

- Semi-hard sell: the retro gets direct and makes the Navigator offer, with the $102 credit, framed around what the member has built and what keeping a coach on their side does for the next six months.
- Fourth retrospective; coach reviews it personally in the call or Discord
- Show the Navigator experience: member spotlights, group coaching moments, stories of members who stayed focused because someone was holding them to it
- Offer is live and stated in the retro, the Journey, email, and Discord; the message is the same everywhere
- Signal: retrospective 4, engagement with Navigator content

## Week 6 — Convert, hard stop day 42

Goal: hard sell, decision made before day 42. The offer from week 5 continues, now with a clear deadline.

- Centerpiece: the Journey radar, week 1 beside week 6. Proof the member generated themselves; the retro script walks them through it.
- Fifth retrospective: the six-week before/after in their own words, with the day-42 goal from the commitment form played back to them
- Clear choice laid out: Navigator (monthly, annual, lifetime) with the $102 credit, or Observer-light with tools and no coaching. Deadline is day 42.
- Day 42: offer closes, state machine completes (Gate 4)
- Signal: conversion rate, billing option chosen, completion rate

## Second track — the Gauntlet for existing members

The same six weeks, run by any Navigator as a refresher or reset. One standard everyone in the house has been through, so it reads as a credential, not a trial.

Why it helps the Observer cohort: Navigators running the Gauntlet sit in the same channels at the same stage, which supplies the belonging weeks 1–4 need. A veteran's third retrospective beside a rookie's is the most persuasive thing a rookie can see.

What stays identical: weeks 2–4 (routine, retrospectives, trend view), the Daily Routine Sheet, the retrospective form, the weekly check-in structure.

What differs:

- Week 1: no orientation. Replace with a reset commitment: why I'm re-running this, what I want to fix, day-42 goal. Same form, different intro copy.
- Weeks 5–6: no conversion campaign. Replace with a finishing arc: a six-week before/after and a public debrief in Discord that the current Observer cohort can see.
- Tracking: the Journey, same as Observers; flag the run as a gauntlet so its numbers stay separate from Observer conversion.

Cadence: rolling and individual. Each member starts on their own day 1 and runs their own 42 days; there are no cohorts. Belonging comes from everyone being somewhere on the same path, not from starting together.

Completion earns a badge in Discord (not a role). How to implement is open: a custom badge via the bot or an existing Discord feature, to be confirmed by the bench.

## Mechanism — behavior scripts for the retro and the Journey

Both the retrospective AI and the Journey accept behavior scripts. By default the retro reads performance since the last retro, checks the member isn't falling off, and reports where they are and whether their true-north heading is sound. The scripts let us give that a six-weeks shape without touching the apps.

- One script per week (1–6) for the retro, closing on three beats: what you did this week, where you stand, what next week asks of you
- A Journey script that knows the member's week, weights the radar accordingly, and opens the first session of each new week with a short welcome: which week this is and what it's for
- The weekly "here's where you are, here's what's next" lives inside the retro, delivered when the member runs it; a check-in message outside the app is a backup, not the primary channel
- Gauntlet track uses the same scripts with the week 1 and weeks 5–6 variants

## Closing method — closers.io

The week 4–6 scripts follow the closers.io psychological path Coach trained in, not improvised persuasion. Coach holds the full closing scripts; next step is to map each stage of that path onto the retro and Journey scripts, where the member talks to an AI rather than a live closer.

Raw material already in the structure: the written day-42 goal (consistency), the week 1 vs week 6 radar (proof in their own numbers), and the day-42 deadline (scarcity).
