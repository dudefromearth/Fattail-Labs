# Wiki — User Guide (all members)

**Where:** `https://labs.fattail.ai/app/wiki`  
**Help (concierge):** `server/help_reference/wiki.md`  
**Spec:** `Specs/FatTail-Labs-Wiki-Spec-v0_2_1.md` (read surface)

The Wiki is the compiled map of what FatTail Labs teaches. Search first, follow
links, hold a concept outside the video. It is not a trading signal and it
never promises a profit.

---

## Open the Wiki

1. Sign in (optional for published pages).
2. **Apps → IKI Lab → Wiki**, or go to `/app/wiki`.
3. Suite pills: **Wiki · IKI Factory · Runner**. Stay on Wiki to read.

## Search first

Type a phrase in your own words. Results live at `/app/wiki/search` with
snippets. You do not need the exact title.

## Start here and recent

**Start here** is a short pinned orientation list. Recent pages are what
changed lately. Neither list ranks trades.

## Read a page

Articles are `/app/wiki/{slug}`. Follow in-text links. **Related** and **In
your practice** on the rail stay hidden until those features fill — empty is
expected, not a broken page.

## Graph

`/app/wiki/graph` shows how pages link. A list appears if the graph is too
large.

## Keyboard

On Wiki routes, **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** jumps by title. Escape closes.

## Published vs drafts

Published pages are readable **without** signing in. Drafts 404 for everyone
except site administrators.

## Wiki vs Help

| | Wiki | Help (emerald, lower right) |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Read the teaching map | Ask about the product, report a bug |
| Writes pages | No | No |

The **Wiki agent** (zinc pill, left of Help) is **administrators only**.
Members never see it. See the Wiki Agent user guide.

## What it is not

Not a broker, not live quotes, not personalised trading advice, not “buy
this.”
