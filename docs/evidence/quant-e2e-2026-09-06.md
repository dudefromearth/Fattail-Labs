## 6. What is assumed, on every response

- **Fill probability 85% per leg-side is a constant.** Not fitted. Era-1 has no depth.
- **Fill placement** uniform on [0.5, 1.0] of the half-spread. Never inside a quarter,
  never through the touch.
- **Legs fill independently.** A real fly is a complex order at a net price. Pessimistic in
  the wrong structure; flagged.
- **Time-to-fill is one snapshot.** Coach: real fills take 10–30 s and can be missed or
  re-seated. That is the fill-friction job (next), and the store's 2 s cadence supports it.
- **Spread, fees, path** are the archive.

`fidelity: era1_no_depth` and `fill_model: unfitted_pessimistic` are on every response until
Sheldon stamps a fitted map (OD-ATRV-6).
