# FatTail Labs — Member Help Knowledge Base

This is the ONLY factual knowledge the help concierge may use about the platform.
It describes FatTail Labs strictly from a member's point of view. If a member's
question is not answerable from this document, the concierge must escalate to the
human support team rather than guess.

## What FatTail Labs is

FatTail Labs (labs.fattail.ai) is the membership education platform for FatTail.
One membership unlocks the course library, live sessions, resources, the community,
and a suite of practice tools that help traders build discipline. The core idea is
"stop the bleeding" — capital preservation and good process first. Guidance here is
about learning and process, never specific financial or profit advice.

## Getting around

- **Home / Hub** — your landing page after signing in; links to courses, live
  sessions, resources and your practice tools.
- **Courses** — the course library. Open a course to see its lessons; your progress
  is tracked as you complete lessons. Some lessons have short quizzes.
- **Lessons** — video + written lessons. A few lessons are free previews; the rest
  need an active membership.
- **Live** — the schedule of live sessions and where to join them. Join links appear
  on the Live page at session time.
- **Resources** — downloadable and reference material.
- **Pathway** — a recommended order to work through the material.
- **Your profile / "Me"** — your account, membership status, and progress.
- **Guide** — the full written User's Guide to the platform.

## Practice tools (the "apps")

- **Trade Log** — record your trades and review them over time.
- **Journal** — guided journaling to reflect on your trading and mindset.
- **Retrospective** — a weekly review ceremony to look back and set intentions.
- **Reports** — summaries and charts of your logged activity.
- **Journey** — tracks your progress and habits across the platform.

Availability of some tools depends on your membership tier.

## Membership

- Tiers are **Observer**, **Activator**, and **Navigator** (Navigator is the most
  complete). Higher tiers unlock more courses, tools, live attention, and support.
- To see current pricing, or to upgrade, change, or cancel, use the **Membership**
  page or your account on the FatTail website — that is where all billing is handled.
- If a course or tool looks locked, it usually means it needs a higher tier than your
  current membership.

## Account & sign-in

- You can sign in with your FatTail Labs email and password, or via the FatTail
  website sign-in.
- Forgot your password? Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page to get a
  reset email.
- Your membership and access come from your purchase on the FatTail website; if you
  just purchased and don't see access yet, signing out and back in usually syncs it.

## Getting help

- This help window is the place to ask questions. The assistant answers common
  questions instantly.
- If the assistant can't help, it hands your question to the human support team and
  you'll be contacted — you can also ask to "talk to a human" at any time.
- You can see your past questions and any team replies in the "My questions" tab.

## Things the assistant does NOT do

- It cannot change your account, membership, billing, or any settings — it can only
  explain how to do things and where to go. Anything that changes your account must
  be done by you on the site or by the support team.
- It does not give personalised financial, trading, or investment advice, and never
  makes profit claims. For trading education, point members to the courses, live
  sessions, and coaching.
