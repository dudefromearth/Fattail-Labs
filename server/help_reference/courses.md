# Courses — what each one teaches

The FatTail Labs course library, from a member's point of view: what each course covers
and roughly what you'll get from it. Open Courses in the navigation to start any of them.
Higher tiers may be needed for some courses. This describes learning content only — never
specific trade or investment advice.

## Recommended order
If you're new, a sensible path is: **Start Here** first (it builds the habits and shows
you around), then the foundations that fit your interest — **0-DTE Foundations** for the
day-trading method, and **FatTail Foundations** for the broader convexity approach — then
**FatTail App** to learn the software, and **Campaigns** to see it all applied live. The
Pathway area also lays out a recommended order.

## Start Here
The onboarding course. It does two things: shows you everything you have access to and how
things work day-to-day, and starts you on a four-week journey to build the foundational
habits of a 0DTE trader. By the end you'll understand the basics of 0DTE strategy and the
core FatTail concepts, and you'll have run a weekly pro-level trading routine yourself —
at least three full Retrospective cycles. Even if you stop after four weeks, you keep the
strategy basics, the concepts, and a weekly routine for life. Best first course for
everyone.

## 0-DTE Foundations
The core training beneath every strategy taught here, focused on 0-DTE (same-day-expiry)
options. Instead of chasing a signal, you learn to read how the 0-DTE market actually
behaves — thinking in probabilities and risk rather than predictions — and to position so
the math is in your favour before you click. Covers: foundational principles (edge from
price, time, and volatility), the morning routine (reading implied volatility, setting
direction and butterfly width, timing entry), the core structures (Classic OTM Butterfly,
Big-Ass Fly, Batman, Time Warp), reading volume profile, risk and profit management, and
running strategies as campaigns. Goal: consistently profitable, independent, pro-level
trading. Expect to set some old habits down.

## FatTail Foundations
The core course on the broader FatTail Protocol — a convexity-first approach for
fat-tailed markets where rare, extreme events drive outcomes. Instead of chasing win rates
or narratives, you learn to structure small, defined-risk positions that benefit from
volatility, regime shifts, and market stress. Teaches asymmetry, optionality, and
antifragility, how tail risk actually works, and how to think in exposure and positioning
across timeframes. It's not about being right — it's about surviving, staying solvent, and
being prepared when the market breaks.

## FatTail App
Platform training for the FatTail App — a dedicated options-analysis tool built for 0DTE
structural trading, where the full trading loop (prepare → execute → reflect) runs end to
end. The course teaches it station by station: the Convexity Heatmap (spot skewed
risk/reward at a glance), the Risk Graph (probability bands, vol modes, dealer-positioning
backdrops, a what-if time machine), the 3D Surface and Price/Time views, submitting orders
against the built-in practice brokerage with zero capital at risk, the Trade Log and
Reports (your book of record), the Journal and Playbook, Alerts, and Vexy (the reasoning
layer). The theme throughout: seeing where convexity exists, not being told what to trade.
The app is in continual development; the course teaches what's live as it actually works.

## Campaigns
A live, evolving course that documents real-world execution of both 0DTE tactical trades
and multi-day FatTail campaigns using the FatTail Protocol. Rather than theory in
isolation, it's a transparent campaign log — showing how convex positions are designed,
deployed, managed, and retired across time horizons and volatility regimes. You'll see how
short-dated tactics integrate with longer-tail structures, how capital is allocated across
campaigns, and how decisions adapt in real time. Applied convexity: optionality over
prediction, discipline over frequency, learning from live exposure. Intermediate level.
