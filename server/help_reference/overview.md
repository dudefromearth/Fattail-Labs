# Overview — FatTail Labs basics

Member-facing basics about FatTail Labs. Describes the platform strictly from a member's
point of view. Guidance here is about learning and process — never specific financial,
trading, or investment advice.

## What FatTail Labs is
FatTail Labs (labs.fattail.ai) is the membership education platform for FatTail. One
membership unlocks the course library, live sessions, resources, the community, and a
suite of practice tools that help traders build discipline. The core idea is "stop the
bleeding" — capital preservation and good process first.

## Membership and tiers
Tiers are Observer, Activator, and Navigator (Navigator is the most complete). Higher
tiers unlock more courses, tools, live attention, and support. To see current pricing, or
to upgrade, change, or cancel, use the Membership page or your account on the FatTail
website — that is where all billing is handled. If a course or tool looks locked, it
usually means it needs a higher tier than your current membership.

## Account and sign-in
You can sign in with your FatTail Labs email and password, or via the FatTail website
sign-in. Forgot your password? Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page to get a
reset email. Your membership and access come from your purchase on the FatTail website; if
you just purchased and don't see access yet, signing out and back in usually syncs it.

## Getting help
This help window is the place to ask questions — the assistant answers common ones
instantly. If it can't help, it hands your question to the human support team and you'll be
contacted; you can also ask to "talk to a human" at any time. You can see your past
questions and any team replies in the "My questions" tab.

## What the assistant cannot do
The assistant cannot change your account, membership, billing, or any settings — it can
only explain how to do things and where to go. Anything that changes your account must be
done by you on the site or by the support team. It does not give personalised financial,
trading, or investment advice, and never makes profit claims. For trading education, it
points you to the courses, live sessions, and coaching.
