# Sessions — the global session clock

A read-only Resources page that shows when major venues are open on one
New York–anchored 23-hour axis (6:00 PM → 5:00 PM ET, skipping the CME
maintenance hour). Open it from Resources → **Sessions**
(`/resource/sessions`). You must be signed in. Observer is included.

## What it is
A clock, not a scanner. It answers “what is trading right now, and what
opens next.” It does not rank hours, name a best session, or show prices.

## The teaching frame
**Morning**, **Afternoon**, and **Closing** are a FatTail frame on the US
cash session (9:30–12:30, 12:30–2:30, 2:30–4:00 ET). They are not
exchange hours. Nothing at the venue changes at 12:30 or 2:30. On an
early close the afternoon truncates at 1:00 PM and Closing is absent.

## What it does not show
Foreign holidays (Lunar New Year, Golden Week, UK bank holidays) are
omitted and can hollow out overnight even when New York is open. Toronto
uses the Canadian calendar and is shown unmodified.

## Controls
Date, Today, Focus (widen around now), Fit (full day). Nothing is saved.
The live marker appears only on today, and not during the CME halt.
