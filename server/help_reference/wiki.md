# Wiki — the compiled map of what we teach

Member-facing guide to the Wiki inside FatTail Labs. The Wiki is a searchable,
cross-linked map of concepts and teaching — not a trading signal, not a chat bot,
and it never promises a profit. Open it from Apps → IKI Lab → Wiki
(`/app/wiki`).

## What the Wiki is
The Wiki is the compiled map of what FatTail Labs teaches — ideas from courses,
live sessions, and practice, written as pages you can search and follow. Use it
when a lesson names a concept you want to hold onto outside the video. Pages
are written in ordinary language and link to each other. The Wiki grows as new
teaching lands; it is a map, not a complete encyclopedia on day one.

## How to open it
From the Hub, open **Apps**, then **IKI Lab**. You land on **Wiki**
(`/app/wiki`). The pills at the top are **Wiki · IKI Factory · Runner** — stay
on Wiki to read the map. You can also go straight to `/app/wiki`.

## Search first
The Wiki home is search-first. Type a phrase in your own words and press Search
(or Enter). Results open at `/app/wiki/search`. Hits show a short snippet so
you can tell if the page is the one you meant. You do not need the exact page
title.

## Start here and recent pages
Below search, **Start here** is a short list of pinned orientation pages — a
sensible first reading list. Recent pages are what was updated lately. Neither
list is a ranking of “best trades.”

## Reading a page
Open a result or a card. The article is at `/app/wiki/{slug}`. Follow highlighted
links in the text to related pages. If a section on the side labelled Related or
In your practice is missing, that is expected for now — those rails are not
filled yet, and empty ones stay hidden.

## Graph
**Graph** (`/app/wiki/graph`) is a picture of how pages link. If there are too
many nodes, you get a simple list instead. Use it to see neighbourhoods, not as
a signal.

## Jump with the keyboard
On Wiki pages, **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux) opens a quick
switcher. Type part of a title and jump. Escape closes it.

## Do I need to sign in?
**Published** pages can be read without signing in. **Drafts** are not public —
if a page is not published yet, you get a not-found page. Signing in does not
unlock hidden draft articles unless you are a site administrator.

## Wiki is not Help
The emerald **Help** button (lower right) is the member concierge: questions
about the product, bugs, and “how do I…”. It does not write Wiki pages. The
Wiki is the map you read. If you cannot find a concept, search the Wiki; if
something in the app is broken or unclear, use Help.

## What the Wiki is not
It is not a broker, not a live quote board, and not personalised trading or
investment advice. It does not tell you what to buy or that a structure will
make money. Process and concepts only.
